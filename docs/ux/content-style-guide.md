# Content style guide

## Voice

Write like a capable community organizer: respectful, direct, calm, and specific. Explain what happens next. Use short sentences and familiar words. Never pressure someone to accept help, compensation, or a meeting.

## Product terms

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Request / help request | Case, ticket, charity case |
| Helper / requester / traveler | Provider/client where it implies employment |
| Proposed compensation | Payment due, guaranteed pay |
| Volunteer preferred; compensation may be discussed | Free unless the requester explicitly selected volunteer/free |
| Email verified / Phone verified | Verified, trusted, vetted, safe helper |
| Shareable summary | Public itinerary |
| Report a concern | Complain about a person |
| Adult traveler | Beneficiary or dependent |

Never imply that the platform checks identity, provides insurance, guarantees safety or payment, or intervenes in an emergency. State that direct compensation and expenses are agreed between adults and are not handled by the platform.

## Safety and privacy copy

- Explain before a user enters details that public summaries omit exact contact and itinerary information.
- Ask a requester to attest to permission before posting for another adult. Do not imply that the platform independently confirmed consent.
- Use neutral correction text: “Please remove phone numbers and booking codes from the public description.”
- For sensitive handoff details, say who can see the information and at what stage.
- Do not put personal details, locations, or message excerpts into push/email previews.
- Show that “Other” is private until a moderator reviews it.
- For an emergency, state that the platform cannot provide emergency response and use the separately approved local guidance.

## Formatting

- Use the user's locale for dates, times, and amounts; include the relevant time zone when a time is specific.
- The initial service area uses EUR. Do not imply conversion or platform payment.
- Use sentence case for headings and buttons. Buttons name the action: “Send offer,” “Confirm terms,” “Request an export.”
- Error messages identify the field and a correction. Confirmation messages state whether anything was sent or saved.
- Keep safety warnings concise and close to the relevant choice.
